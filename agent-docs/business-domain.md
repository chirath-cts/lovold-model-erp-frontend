# Business Domain

## Company Context
Lovold operates in a B2B aquaculture context with equipment, components, and operational products.

## Core ERP Needs Reflected in Phase 1
- Customer-specific pricing control
- Sales order lifecycle visibility
- Profit visibility
- Dashboarding for business validation
- Product and category catalog browsing

## Deferred to Phase 2
- Inventory tracking
- Procurement
- Supplier onboarding
- Warehouse logistics
- Stock thresholds and low-inventory alerts
- Order status history and audit trail
