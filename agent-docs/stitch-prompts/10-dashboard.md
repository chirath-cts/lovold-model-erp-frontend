# Prompt 10: Dashboard

Paste this last among the main functional sections.

```md
Continue the Lovold ERP project and preserve the established app shell and UI patterns.

Now design the Dashboard as the top-level executive and operational overview for Lovold ERP.

The Dashboard must balance commercial metrics and operational risk.

Core KPI requirements:

- total sales
- total orders
- estimated profit
- delayed orders
- orders in production
- orders awaiting materials
- active customers
- inbound due soon

Operational panels should include:

- recent orders
- delayed orders
- upcoming inbound supplier purchase orders
- low available stock items
- orders by status
- ETA risk orders
- top customers
- top sellable items

Use domain-relevant example content so the dashboard feels like an aquaculture engineering business:

- high-value project orders
- spare-part replenishment orders
- feed-system components
- sensor and monitoring hardware
- customers operating salmon farms in Norway, Chile, Scotland, Iceland, Canada, and the Faroe Islands

Important business rules:

- the Dashboard should reflect products and components together where appropriate
- top sellable items must combine products and components
- inventory visibility should focus on:
  - low available stock
  - high reserved stock
  - component shortage risk
- delayed orders depend on stored promised ETA
- inbound timing must be visible as part of operational risk

The dashboard should answer these questions quickly:

- How is the business performing?
- Which orders need attention now?
- What inventory or inbound issues are threatening commitments?
- What is stuck in production or waiting for materials?

The `Today`, `Week`, and `Month` controls may exist visually but do not need to be functional in this phase.

Design this screen to feel like the command center of the whole product.
```
